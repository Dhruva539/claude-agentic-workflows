# Quiz formats

Load this file only when running a quiz.

## Scenario question
Describe a short real-world situation, then ask "which option fits best and why?" Give 4 options (A-D).

## Spot the mistake
Show a small snippet or design with one flaw. Ask me to find it.

## Explain back
Ask me to explain the concept in my own words in 2 sentences. Then correct gaps.

## Difficulty ladder
- Beginner: definitions and "what is".
- Intermediate: scenarios and trade-offs.
- Advanced: debugging and design choices under constraints.
